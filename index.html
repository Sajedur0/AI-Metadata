    Why do platforms show AI Info?

    Platforms scan uploads for C2PA and XMP — the usual file-level trigger for “AI Info” and “Made with AI.” This tool re-encodes pixels in your browser so those containers are gone. Your files never leave this device.

    What we remove

    • C2PA / Content Credentials
    • XMP generation tags (Midjourney, Firefly, Stable Diffusion)
    • EXIF: GPS, camera, timestamps, serials
    • PNG tEXt / iTXt / zTXt chunks

    FAQ

    Is anything uploaded?

    No. Decoding, stripping, and download all happen in this tab.

    Will pixels change?

    JPEG/WebP are re-encoded at high quality. PNG is lossless. AVIF exports as PNG.

    Does this hide visual watermarks?

    No. We only strip file-level metadata. Pixel watermarks and model fingerprints stay.
